Our founding practice

Immigration & asylum law, since day one.

The firm began in 2011 doing this work, for people who had arrived with nothing and had no one to speak for them. It is the practice everything else was built around, and it is still the largest part of what we do.

Asylum & humanitarian protection

Fresh claims, substantive interviews, and protection based on persecution, sexual orientation, gender or religion.

Appeals & refusals

First-tier and Upper Tribunal appeals against Home Office refusals, and onward challenges where the law has been misapplied.

Detention & bail

Urgent bail applications, removal directions, and challenges to unlawful detention. This work is time-critical — call, don't email.

Family reunion

Bringing a spouse, partner or children to join a family member already granted protection in the UK.

Spouse, partner & family visas

Applications, extensions and refusals under Appendix FM, including where the financial requirement is in issue.

Settlement & citizenship

Indefinite leave to remain, long-residence applications, naturalisation and registration of children as British citizens.

EU Settlement Scheme

Late applications, status upgrades from pre-settled to settled, and refusals or curtailments under the scheme.

Work, study & sponsorship

Skilled Worker and student routes, sponsor licence applications, and compliance for employers who sponsor staff.

Judicial review

Challenging Home Office decisions in the Court of Session where there is no right of appeal.

Deportation & removal

Resisting deportation, including on Article 8 family and private life grounds, and revocation of deportation orders.

Time limits

Immigration deadlines are short and strict

Appeal windows can be as little as 14 days from the date on the decision letter, and a missed deadline is very hard to undo. If a letter has arrived, bring it to us before the date on it passes.

Legal aid

You may not have to pay for this.

We are registered with the Scottish Legal Aid Board (SLAB). Depending on the type of your case and your financial position, you may qualify for legal aid. If you qualify, our fees and the outlays incurred are met by SLAB rather than by you.

Legal aid is available for much of our immigration, asylum, criminal, family, mental health and children's hearing work. We will check whether you qualify at the first conversation, before any agreement is made — and we will tell you plainly if you don't.

Who we are

People are at the heart of everything we do — which sounds like something every firm says, so here is what it actually means for us.

Maguire Solicitors was established in Glasgow in 2011. The business began by fighting for equality and basic human rights through immigration and asylum law, often for people who had arrived with nothing and had no one to speak for them. That is not a founding story we have grown out of. It is still the measure we hold the rest of the firm against.

Over a decade later we have widened our legal offering, strengthened our team to more than thirty people, and built a practice that works with leading organisations across the UK and around the world. The range has changed. The way we treat the person in front of us has not.

We also understand the power of reassurance, and the difference a comforting word makes at the moment you feel most alone. It costs nothing to talk to us, and we would rather you called too early than too late.

And we have an urge to give something back. We work with local and international charities, channelling our time and our talents to help protect the most vulnerable. Whether your challenges are personal or professional, at home or away — with Maguire Solicitors, we're in this together.
